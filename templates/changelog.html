Changelog

Every notable change to CloudChat, newest first. Defaults below can be changed by whoever runs the server.

🧭 Coming next

Planned, in no particular order. No dates promised.

  • More languages — starting with Chinese.
  • Install as an app — add CloudChat to your home screen and launch it like a native app.
  1. October 9, 2026 · v1.4

    🔌 Automatic reconnect & hardening

    • Automatic reconnect. If your connection drops or we restart the server, the room shows "Reconnecting…" and picks up where you left off — no refresh, nothing lost.
    • Fair-use limits keep spam and abuse from slowing everyone else down.
    • No third parties. Fonts are now served by CloudChat itself, so pages make no requests to other companies.
    • Stricter security headers, and our server logs no longer contain room or note IDs.
  2. October 8, 2026 · v1.3

    🛡️ Privacy controls & a new homepage

    • Late joiners start fresh. By default, people who join a room only see messages sent after they arrived. Refreshing or reconnecting still restores everything you've already seen.
    • Rooms destroy themselves. Five minutes after the last person leaves, the room, its messages and its files are deleted. Come back within that window and nothing is lost.
    • See who's online. A live counter and member list in the room header. Several tabs from the same browser count as one person.
    • Accurate presence after restarts. Members who dropped off without saying goodbye are cleared automatically within about a minute.
    • New homepage with a clear explanation of what we store and for how long, plus an FAQ.
  3. October 8, 2026 · v1.2

    📱 Built for phones

    • The chat room now fills the whole screen on phones, with a compact one-line header.
    • No more surprise zoom when tapping the message box on iPhone, and the input bar stays clear of the home indicator.
    • Images show as small thumbnails and open in a full-screen preview instead of a new tab.
    • A new app icon, and links back to the homepage from every entry page.
  4. October 8, 2026 · v1.1

    📎 Files, invite links & message history

    • Share files and images up to 10 MB — pick them, drag them in, or paste a screenshot.
    • Invite links. Copy one link to bring people in; paste a link or a room ID to join.
    • Message history. Refreshing the page no longer means starting from a blank screen.
    • Fixes: no more duplicate messages after a room is re-entered, nobody can post under someone else's name, and long messages (up to 2,000 characters) no longer drop the connection.
  5. October 8, 2026

    🔒 Secret notes are live

    Send password-protected, self-destructing notes. Your note is encrypted in your browser before it leaves your device — our servers never see the text or the password. Choose a TTL of 1 day, 1 week, or 1 month. The note is gone the moment it's read, after 5 wrong password attempts, or when the timer runs out.

    Send a secret →
  6. May 21, 2026 · v1.0

    🚀 CloudChat launches

    Instant, disposable chat rooms with no accounts. Built with Go (Gin), Redis Pub/Sub so it can run on several servers at once, and Vue 3 + Tailwind CSS for the interface.
